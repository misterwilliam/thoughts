# Introduction

Hello! Welcome to my page on my philosophical thoughts. It is my personal passion project.

I have a big interest in AI and ethics. My main interest is in what ethics we should try
to install in AI because I think that is the most promising path towards AI safety. I
think that we should give AI universal human morals. I don't believe we know what those
are and the key task is to work on improving our collective understanding of them. This is
the key topic I try to explore in my essays.

## Recommended reading order

A recommended reading order is to approach the essays in the following order:

1. [AI Danger](ai_danger.md)
2. [What are morals?](morals.md)
3. [Why we should value one another](valuing_one_another.md)

In this order I first define what I think is the most dangerous AI scenario, and why I
think teaching AI ethics is the best approach. Then I explore my perspective on ethics. I
don't solve ethics, but I try to explain what I think is a good direction.

## Acknowledgements

I am fully aware that many of the ideas I discuss are not my unique invention, and I do
very poor job of acknowledging the prior (very well known) thinkers whose ideas I am
regurgitating. My excuses are:

1. I would like discuss these topics with as plain language as possible, and bringing in
   prior work is challenging without introducing a lot extra technical terminology.
2. Properly acknowledging prior work takes a lot of effort and threatens to introduce many
   tangents. There is a lot I would like to cover, and time spent on that competes with
   time spent articulating the ideas that I want to discuss. Every new idea introduced,
   brings a whole host of other ideas and I am trying to keep things as simple as
   possible.
3. Modern LLMs do an excellent job in discussing prior work.

Finally I am just an amateur. While I do understand that many of the ideas I discuss have
been well explored by others before me, I don't understand those explorations well enough
to do a good job incorporating those explorations in my essays.

I would like to thank Claude. I've had substantial conversations with Claude on the
contents of my essays and greatly benefited from its feedback.
